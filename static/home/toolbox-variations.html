Tools of choice

Ten treatments of one new section for the end of the home page: eleven tools in four groups (IDE, agents, terminal, utilities), every one wearing its real mark. Built from the site's own tokens: neutral-100 page, slate ink, #3429ff accent, Rosé Dusk in the dark, the hero terminal palette, and the command palette chrome. Toggle dark mode; both states ship. Pick a number and it becomes the real section.

Editorial prose

One paragraph, every tool linked inline with its mark. The most human treatment and the cheapest to maintain.

Tools of Choice

Most of my day runs through a short list of tools. Designs start in Figma and ship from VS Code, with Claude Code and Codex doing the heavy lifting on branches. The terminal is Ghostty, dressed up by Oh My Posh and never far from OpenCode. Everything worth sharing goes through CleanShot or gets squeezed into a GIF by Gifski, Arc handles the browsing, and Raycast glues the whole thing together.
