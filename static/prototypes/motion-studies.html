Motion with a pulse.

Six generative backgrounds driven by Perlin noise and layered drift — no keyframes, no loops, nothing linear. Each one wanders like weather and never plays the same frame twice.

Move your cursor over a frame: every scene answers with a slow, eased parallax. Honors reduced-motion preferences.

01Nebula Drift

Four soft chromatic clouds wander on independent noise paths — indigo, coral, violet, and a trace of cyan. They breathe, cross, and separate like slow weather; the pointer leans the whole sky a few pixels.

02Undercurrent

A few hundred near-invisible particles ride a curl-noise flow field, leaving faint teal trails that swirl and dissolve. The current itself slowly changes course, so the pattern is always composing itself.

03Silk Ribbons

Three translucent ribbons undulate across the top third, each sampling its own strip of noise. The waves interfere and phase past each other — closer to fabric in wind than to an oscillator.

04Firefly Dust

Sparse motes drift on noise with individual eased glow cycles — every spark has its own tempo and depth, and nearer ones parallax harder against the pointer. Quietest of the set.

05Still Water

Rings bloom from a slowly wandering origin, expanding with a long ease-out like drops on still water. Warm, architectural, and almost meditative — motion you feel more than see.

06Night Aurora

A dark-mode direction: curtains of blue-violet light sway on stacked noise octaves over a sparse starfield. Cinematic without ever competing with the type.
