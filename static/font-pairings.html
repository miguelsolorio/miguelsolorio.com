Font pairings on the home page

The live home page below, with a pairing injected over it — hero, section headings, and the wordmark take the display face, prose and cards the body face, the terminal the monospace. Only the faces (and their weight and variable axes) are swapped: sizes, tracking, and the hero's hard-coded line breaks stay exactly as the site sets them, so a wider face shows itself by running long rather than by being re-typeset. Nothing is mocked up — it is / in a frame, so it stays current as the page changes. Shortlisted: 02, 07, 15.
