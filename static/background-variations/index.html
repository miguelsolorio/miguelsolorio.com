Ten ways to add depth near the top of the portfolio using soft gradients, tactile noise, and motion that stays behind the work.

01Aurora Wash

Soft indigo and coral clouds. Broad, slow movement makes it feel polished without calling attention to itself.

02Electric Horizon

A cool blue horizon line, sparse signal lights, and generous empty space. Cleanest of the animated options.

03Chromatic Mesh

A richer mesh of blue, pink, and a trace of yellow. Most expressive while still keeping the center readable.

04Citrus Bloom

Warm orange, rose, and violet with a radial detail. Friendly and editorial rather than overtly technical.

05Blueprint Dust

A fading technical grid and tiny data particles. The most direct nod to tools, systems, and building software.

06Prism Beam

Translucent diagonal light beams drift behind the hero. Geometric, crisp, and a little futuristic.

07Solar Flare

A vivid sunrise palette with an orbital form. Energetic and memorable, with the strongest visual personality.

08Orbital Mist

Frosted, dimensional orbs in a pale lavender field. Playful motion with a product-render sensibility.

09Quiet Bands

A restrained lavender fade with hairline bands. Nearly static and especially friendly to the typography.

10Midnight Signal

A cinematic dark-mode-first direction with blue-violet light and sparse stars. Highest contrast of the set.
