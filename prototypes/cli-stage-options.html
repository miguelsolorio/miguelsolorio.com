CLI stage — 10 gradient pair options

Candidate surface + artwork pairs for the cli-agents embeds, each shown light and dark on a static stand-in of the topography ambient. All keep the theme's bloom geometry so any pick drops straight into project.css as a [data-project-theme="cli"] override. The mini terminal casts each option's shadow tint. Reply with a number.

1

Terminal Mint

Terminal green — kin to the diff rows in every scene, and no other surface on the site is green.

2

Seafoam Teal

Cooler, more aquatic green — nods to terminal cyan without drifting back toward the ink's blue.

3

Sage

The quietest green — barely past neutral, keeps the drawn map calm and lets the scenes lead.

4

Champagne Sand

Warm gray-gold neutral — the farthest hue from the blurple contour ink, so the stage always separates.

5

Coral Dawn

Rose-coral warmth — the strongest color statement of the set, glows against the cool slate field.

6

Phosphor Amber

CRT-amber homage — terminal heritage in a hue nothing else on the site claims anymore.

7

Neutral Slate

What's live right now — no pigment, elevation does the work. Pick this to affirm the status quo.

8

Ink Terminal

The stage itself becomes a screen — deep ink in both modes, and the white scenes pop hard off it.

9

Powder Steel

The old blue rebuilt — desaturated toward steel so it reads as material, not as the ink's blue.

10

Mint → Sand

A two-hue crossfade — mint enters, warm sand answers — bridging the cool map and the warm content.

Contour rings here are a still approximation of the shader; judge hue and value, not the drawing. Shadow tints follow the single.css formula (.26/.18 light, .42/.3 dark).
